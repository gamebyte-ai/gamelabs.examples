- [ ] `public/assets/audio/sfx_click.mp3` - 0.5s - Soft, crisp UI button click with a subtle electronic beep, played when tapping buttons (Restart, Next, etc.).

## Actions
- [ ] `public/assets/audio/sfx_slide.mp3` - 0.8s - Light bouncy slide sound with an airy whoosh, played when a block successfully slides off the board.
- [ ] `public/assets/audio/sfx_blocked.mp3` - 0.4s - Soft failure sound, not harsh, slight descending tone/buzz, played when a block is clicked but its path is blocked.

## Feedback
- [ ] `public/assets/audio/sfx_win.mp3` - 2.0s - Bright, cheerful success sound with a sparkle and chime, played when the level is completed and the last block exits.

## Music
- [ ] `public/assets/audio/bgm_gameplay.mp3` - 30.0s - Upbeat, energetic, light background music loop for a casual puzzle game, played continuously during gameplay.
